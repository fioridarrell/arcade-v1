Arcade V1 is beta software with no independent security audit yet. Do not use funds you can't afford to lose. Do not restore a seed phrase that holds real savings. It installs a background service that starts at login and connects to other arcades.

The service starts when you log in and connects to other arcades. It runs as you, not as Administrator. It keeps a wallet on this PC. Read SECURITY.md in the Arcade V1 repository before you put coins you care about in it:

https://github.com/martinseeger2002/arcade-v1/blob/main/SECURITY.md

Security problems: team@arcade-nodes.com

Nothing is installed until you check the box on this screen.
